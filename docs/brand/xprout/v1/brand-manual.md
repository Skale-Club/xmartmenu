10 / HANDOFF
The files and when to use them
Start with the supplied artwork. Keep its proportions, clear space and approved colors intact.
svg/
Scalable outlined logos, with transparent and solid-background versions.
png/
Transparent 256/512 px exports and prepared background versions.
icons/
App icons, avatars, maskable icons, favicon and a social card.
print/
Vector PDF logo plates in RGB, for printer conversion and proofing.
fonts/
Original support fonts with their complete OFL license files.
manual/
This guide and its editable text reference.
source/
Approved symbol, outlined geometry and reproducible build scripts.
The approved v7 symbol path is preserved exactly in every exported composition. New spacing and typography guidance in this manual is consolidated for brand pack v1.
Application rollout is a separate step.
