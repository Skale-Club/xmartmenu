Xprout ("**Xprout**", "**we**", "**us**") is operated by Skale Club LLC. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect when you use xprout.app and the digital menus, ordering and related services we provide to restaurants (the "**Service**"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

The Service has two kinds of users:

- **Restaurants**: businesses that create an account to publish menus, take orders and use our tools ("**Restaurants**").
- **Guests**: people who open a Restaurant's menu, for example by scanning a QR code or tapping an NFC tag, and may sign in, place orders or chat with a menu assistant ("**Guests**").

## 1. Our role

When a Restaurant uses the Service to serve its Guests, the Restaurant decides why and how Guest information is used, and we process that information on the Restaurant's behalf as its service provider. Each Restaurant's own privacy practices apply to the information it receives. If you are a Guest and want to exercise a privacy right over information a Restaurant holds, contact that Restaurant; we will help it respond.

For our own website, Restaurant accounts and the people who request access to Xprout, we decide how information is used.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you give us

- **Restaurant accounts and access requests**: name, email address, phone number, restaurant name and details, address, and the messages you send us.
- **Menu content**: dishes, descriptions, prices, ingredients, allergen and dietary labels, photos, videos and 3D models that a Restaurant uploads or generates with our tools.
- **Guest sign-in**: your phone number (verified with a one-time code sent by SMS) or, if you choose to continue with Google, your Google account name, email address and profile picture.
- **Orders**: the items you choose, notes, table or pickup details, your name and phone number, and payment status. Card details are entered directly with our payment processor; we do not receive or store full card numbers.
- **Menu assistant**: the questions you type or speak to a Restaurant's AI menu assistant, when the Restaurant has turned it on. Voice messages are converted to text to answer them.

### Information collected automatically

- **Menu usage**: which menus, categories and dishes are viewed, how a menu was opened (for example a QR scan), session timing, language and device type. We use this to give Restaurants statistics about their own menus.
- **Website analytics**: on Xprout's own public pages (our home page, pricing and blog) we use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how visitors use our site. Clarity records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling and page layout) with all text masked. These tools are **not** loaded on Restaurant menus, sign-in pages or account dashboards.
- **Technical data**: IP address, browser and device information, and logs needed to run, secure and debug the Service.

### Camera and 3D views

When you view a dish in 3D on your table, your phone's camera is used by your device's own augmented-reality feature. The camera image stays on your device; we do not receive or store it.

## 3. How we use information

- To provide the Service: publish menus, sign you in, send orders to the Restaurant, process payments and show order status.
- To operate Restaurant accounts, review access requests, provide support and send service messages.
- To give Restaurants statistics about their menus and orders.
- To improve, secure and debug the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce our Terms of Service.
- To run optional AI features a Restaurant turns on, such as the menu assistant, menu translation and image or 3D model generation.
- To comply with law.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 4. How we share information

- **With the Restaurant** whose menu you use: your orders, the details you give with them, and menu usage for that Restaurant.
- **With service providers** that help us run the Service, under contracts that limit their use of the information: hosting and infrastructure (Hetzner, Cloudflare), authentication and SMS verification (Google Firebase / Identity Platform), payments (Stripe), AI processing (OpenRouter and the AI model providers it routes to), messaging and notifications (such as Telegram for internal alerts), website analytics (Google Analytics, Google Tag Manager, Microsoft Clarity), and error monitoring (Sentry).
- **For legal reasons**: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, Restaurants, the public or Xprout.
- **In a business transfer**: if Xprout or Skale Club LLC is involved in a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, under this Policy's protections.

## 5. Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and local storage that are necessary to sign you in, keep your cart and preferences, and protect the Service. On Xprout's public website pages, Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity set analytics cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; necessary cookies are required for sign-in and ordering to work. Learn more about [how Google uses information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites) and the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

## 6. Data retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service and for the purposes described here. Restaurant account data is kept while the account is active and deleted or anonymized within a reasonable time after it closes, except where we must keep it longer for legal, tax or security reasons. Order records are kept as the Restaurant and the law require. Website analytics data is kept for up to 14 months.

## 7. Security

We use administrative, technical and physical safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls and encrypted backups. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. California residents have the right to know, delete and correct personal information and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights; we do not sell or share personal information. To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity before responding. If your information was collected by a Restaurant, we may refer your request to that Restaurant.

## 9. Children

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers may process information in other countries. When information is transferred, we take steps to protect it as described in this Policy and as required by law.

## 11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date. If changes are significant, we will give additional notice, for example in the Restaurant dashboard.
